How it works

  1. Recruitment 1, intake form. A candidate applies with a PDF CV. The form is validated, duplicates are caught, the CV is saved to Google Drive and the application is logged in a Google Sheet.
  2. Recruitment 2, AI screening. Every hour it reads new applications, scores each CV with AI, then emails an invitation or a decline. Borderline cases are sent to HR for review.
  3. Recruitment 3, error handler. Logs failures, emails alerts, sends a daily digest and checks that the queue keeps moving.
  4. Job Application Coach. Reads a CV against a job post and emails a coaching report with a CV match, a cover letter and interview preparation.
  5. RAG HR assistant. Loads the policy documents from Google Drive at start-up and every morning at 7:00. It answers questions only from those documents.

The system is live only while the host computer, Docker, n8n and ngrok are running. When they are off, the badge shows Offline and the form buttons are disabled.
