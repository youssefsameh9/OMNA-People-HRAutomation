How it works

WorkflowTriggerWhat it does
1. Recruitment 1 - Intake FormFormCandidates apply with a PDF CV; it is validated, duplicates are caught, the CV is saved to Drive and the application is logged to a sheet
2. Recruitment 2 - AI ScreeningEvery hourReads new applications, scores each CV with AI, emails invites or declines, and sends borderline cases to HR
3. Recruitment 3 - Error HandlerError + schedulesLogs failures, emails alerts, sends a daily digest, checks the queue is moving
4. Job Application CoachFormReads a CV against a job post and emails a coaching report
5. RAG HR assistantChat, n8n start, daily, manualLoads the policy documents from Google Drive and answers only from them
